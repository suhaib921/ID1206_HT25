# Seminar 1: Real-time Operating Systems and Scheduling

## Preparation Checklist

To ensure you are prepared for the group discussion and presentation, please complete the following reading list before Seminar 1.

### Reading List

*   **Enea Linux Real-time Guide:** Read sections 2.1 - 2.3, and 2.6.
    *   [Link to Guide](https://linux.enea.com/4.0/documentation/html/book-enea-linux-realtime-guide)

*   **Kernel.org - Scheduler Design (CFS):** Understand the Completely Fair Scheduler.
    *   [Link to documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/scheduler/sched-design-CFS.html)

*   **Kernel.org - Scheduler RT Group:** Learn about real-time scheduling groups.
    *   [Link to documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/scheduler/sched-rt-group.html)

*   **Operating System Concepts:** Study Section 6.5.4 on Priority Inversion.

*   **Mars Pathfinder Spacecraft Software Incident:** Understand a real-world example of real-time software challenges.
    *   [Link to Article](https://www.rapitasystems.com/blog/what-really-happened-software-mars-pathfinder-spacecraft)

*   **Kernel.org - Real-time Linux Theory:** Explore the theoretical aspects of Real-time Linux.
    *   [Link to documentation](https://www.kernel.org/doc/html/next/core-api/real-time/theory.html)
